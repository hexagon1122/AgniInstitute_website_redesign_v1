Agni Institute and Agni Yoga

How the Institute relates to the Agni Yoga teachings.

Join a free intro session

Offerings to the public of Agni Yoga publications, as well as preservation and dissemination of writings, are under way. This includes dialogues focused on deepening an understanding of Agni Yoga and realizing its pursuit as a natural part of daily life.

Activities include ongoing discussions on practicing inner yogas, broadening of consciousness, and the blossoming of heart and awareness. Understanding, developing and sustaining methods of meditation and personal striving are provided through discussions, periodic sittings and meditation retreats. Ongoing mentoring and support are a foundational part of this work.

Inspiration for research is drawn from concepts and suggestions in the texts of Agni Yoga, and investigations may also include explorations motivated by ethics and values in accord with those expressed in Agni Yoga.

Independence

Although we at Agni Institute take inspiration from the writings and teachings of Agni Yoga, the Institute is not officially affiliated with any organization. The Agni Yoga texts are provided free of charge by their copyright holders on the sole condition that no modifications are introduced by any third party.
