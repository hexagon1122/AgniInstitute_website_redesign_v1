Institute Research

Specialized research based on investigations suggested in the texts of Agni Yoga.

Join a free intro session

Contained in the texts of Agni Yoga are hundreds of suggestions and indications regarding research into natural law and the nature of consciousness. In the internal research of the organization, co-workers at Agni Institute collate these suggestions and write research and test protocols.

Laboratory space and equipment currently used by the Quantal Research group support benchwork such as fabricating fixtures and circuits needed for measurements and data collection. A space newly constructed in 2024 is also used for these and related projects.

An initial investigation is being designed by two scientists in the organization and pursued over the course of 2025. Work is expected in the Institute lab as well as at regional universities. Depending on the nature of the work, some methods may also migrate to Collaborative Research so citizen and professional scientists can take part.

Agni Institute welcomes new team members in these efforts and anticipates reporting on and sharing findings with the public.
