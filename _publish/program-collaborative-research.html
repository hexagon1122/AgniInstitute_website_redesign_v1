Collaborative Research

Engage in experiments and investigations, collaborate internationally, and report results so all can benefit.

Join a free intro session

Thousands of suggestions for beneficial research are present in the texts of Agni Yoga. The research group at Agni Institute works to establish and carry out viable experiments that verify aspects of nature alluded to in the texts and seek beneficial applications.

In collaborative programs, participants working independently follow a defined method, then contribute and share methods, data, and findings. Participation is not limited to any one region; the approach is intended to be open-source and global in scope. Mentoring is expected as a standard part of exchange.

Research is offered at three levels of skill and experience:

  • Enthusiast
  • Citizen scientist
  • Professional and/or academic investigator

Volunteers may grow skills such as designing research projects and experiments, collecting and presenting data, writing papers, using tools and instrumentation, CAD and solid-printing for fixtures, and related laboratory practice. Some methods developed in Institute Research may also migrate into these collaborative programs.
