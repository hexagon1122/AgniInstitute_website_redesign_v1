Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026.

Join a free intro session

This policy explains what information the Agni Institute website collects and how it is used. We collect as little as we need.

Contact form

When you submit our contact form, the name, email address, subject and message you enter are sent to us through Netlify Forms, a service of our website host, Netlify. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and keep a record of our correspondence. We do not sell or rent it, and we do not add you to a mailing list without your permission.

Spam protection (Google reCAPTCHA)

Our forms are protected by Google reCAPTCHA, provided through Netlify, to block automated spam. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, such as device and browser data, and sends it to Google for analysis. Your use of reCAPTCHA is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Google Ads tags and cookies

This website uses the Google tag (gtag.js) for Google Ads. It helps us understand whether our ads lead people to our site and measure "conversions", such as a contact form being submitted or the donate button being clicked. Google may set cookies or use similar technologies and receive information such as your IP address, browser and device details, the pages you visit and how you arrived. Google's use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage ad personalization at Google Ad Settings or block cookies in your browser.

Donations

Donations are processed by PayPal on PayPal's own site. We do not see or store your card details. PayPal shares with us the information needed to record and acknowledge your gift. See the PayPal Privacy Statement.

Hosting and logs

Our website is hosted by Netlify, which may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs to deliver and secure the site.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have sent us by contacting info@agniinstitute.org.
