Get Involved

Everyone at Agni Institute is a volunteer. Many are participants. Many are both.

Join a free intro session

Volunteer and participate

As a participant you can engage in studies, workshops, exercises, discussion groups, classes, research and explorations of consciousness. Expand your perception, abilities and understanding of who and what each of us is. We welcome all who are sincerely interested in understanding the nature of consciousness and reality, including in research projects, some of which involve laboratory settings, methods and instrumentation.

As a volunteer you can work with like-minded people to build projects and programs that advance human understanding and capability.

Meditate

Still the mind, find inner peace, increase focus, clarity and creativity, and begin to realize your higher nature. At Agni Institute, meditation is taught and practiced as the principal skill for investigating the nature of consciousness, existence and reality. Whether you are just starting or have meditated for many years, we offer practical support, discussions and sessions. See Meditation and our beginner's guide.

Investigate

We not only read about the natural world but want to experience it first hand. Drawing on the books of Agni Yoga and others, the Institute designs investigations and experiments into correlations between consciousness and the natural world, and invites participation at every level, from the enthusiast looking for hands-on activities to the trained professional investigator. See Collaborative Research.

Learn

As a participant or volunteer, you could learn or add skills in:

  • Meditation
  • Dreaming methods
  • Designing research projects and experiments
  • Design, including CAD software
  • Collecting, analyzing and presenting data
  • Writing papers reporting findings
  • Building test fixtures and experimental apparatus
  • Solid-printing research parts
  • Instrumentation used in experiments

Dream

The Subtle Realm group explores the nearly one-third of our lives spent asleep: how consciousness and physiology shape dreaming, and how to develop conscious activity in the dream state. See Subtle Realm and Lucid Dreaming.

Donate

Financial contributions help fund public outreach, community events and classes, research equipment and software, and basic administrative costs. Make a donation.

In keeping with long-standing tradition, there is no charge for any of our activities or programs. Contact us to get started.
